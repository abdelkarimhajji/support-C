Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this app root my phone?
A: No. This app is a utility to verify if your phone is already rooted. It does not modify system files.

Q: Why do I see ads?
A: Ads help support the development of the app. You can remove them by upgrading to the Pro version in the Upgrades tab.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Root Checker Basic does not collect personal data. Any device information checked (like Model or Android Version) is processed locally on your device.
