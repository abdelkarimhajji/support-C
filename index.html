Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I play the Daily Challenge?
A: Every day, a new deal is available. Complete it to earn a crown! You can find the Daily Challenge in the main menu.

Q: Is the game free?
A: Yes, Spider Solitaire is completely free to play. Ads help support the development of the app so we can keep improving it.

Q: How do I start a new game?
A: You can start a new deal anytime by tapping the menu button and selecting "New Game."

Privacy Policy
